# India Financial Sector Regulatory Reference Catalogue

> REFERENCE CATALOGUE. Each entry below is a document-level pointer to a real law, regulatory direction or industry standard, with a short scope description written in ReqPilot's own words. Entries contain no regulatory text, clause numbers or paragraph references. Issuers periodically amend, consolidate or repeal their instruments, so always confirm the current version on the issuer's website before relying on it.

## REF-01 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
Topics: consent_privacy, data_retention, breach_incident_reporting
Official title: The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
Issuing body: Parliament of India; administered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
Date: Enacted August 2023. Implementing rules are being brought into force in phases; verify current commencement status with the issuer.
Obtain from: https://www.meity.gov.in (also https://www.indiacode.nic.in)
Scope: India's general law on the processing of digital personal data. It sets out the obligations of organisations that decide how personal data is processed, the basis on which processing may take place, and the rights available to individuals whose data is processed. It also establishes a regulatory body to handle breaches and complaints.
Reference entry only: this summary is not regulatory text. Obtain the official document from the issuer and upload it to the Knowledge Base for clause-level evidence.

## REF-02 Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, 2011
Topics: encryption, consent_privacy, access_control, breach_incident_reporting
Official title: The Information Technology Act, 2000; and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
Issuing body: Parliament of India (Act) and Government of India, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (Rules)
Date: Act of 2000, substantially amended in 2008; Rules notified in April 2011
Obtain from: https://www.meity.gov.in (also https://www.indiacode.nic.in)
Scope: The Act provides the legal framework for electronic records, digital signatures and cyber offences in India. The SPDI Rules describe expectations for organisations handling sensitive personal information, including maintaining reasonable security practices. Applicability of the SPDI Rules should be checked against the commencement of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
Reference entry only: this summary is not regulatory text. Obtain the official document from the issuer and upload it to the Knowledge Base for clause-level evidence.

## REF-04 RBI Cyber Security Framework in Banks
Topics: audit_logging, breach_incident_reporting, access_control, business_continuity
Official title: Cyber Security Framework in Banks (RBI circular)
Issuing body: Reserve Bank of India
Date: June 2016
Obtain from: https://www.rbi.org.in (Notifications section)
Scope: Requires banks to put in place a board-approved cyber security policy and a cyber crisis management plan distinct from the broader IT policy. It covers continuous surveillance through a security operations centre, protection of customer information, and reporting of cyber security incidents to the Reserve Bank.
